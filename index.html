Why healthy air matters

Healthy air supports the people who depend on your space.

~90% of our time is spent indoors, on average.

Healthier indoor environments can reduce exposure to pollutants and support health, comfort and performance.

Evidence: U.S. EPA indoor-air guidance, EPA school-performance review and a multicountry study of indoor air and cognitive performance.

01 Support respiratory health

Reduce exposure to particles, allergens, dampness and common asthma and allergy triggers.

02 Support focus, learning and performance

Better ventilation and lower indoor pollutants can support concentration and cognitive performance.

03 Improve comfort. Protect the space.

Manage humidity, odors, stuffiness and moisture before they become larger concerns.
